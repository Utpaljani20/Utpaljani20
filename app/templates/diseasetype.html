Skin Diseases

Explore common conditions, their symptoms, and treatments. Early detection is key to effective management.

Acne

Acne occurs when hair follicles become plugged with oil and dead skin cells. It causes whiteheads, blackheads, or pimples.

Acne Scars

Usually the result of inflamed blemishes caused by skin pores engorged with excess oil, dead cells, and bacteria.

Acanthosis Nigricans

A condition that causes dark discoloration in body folds and creases, typically affecting the neck and armpits.

Melasma

A condition that causes dark discoloration in body folds and creases, typically affecting the neck and armpits.

Vitiligo

A condition that causes loss of skin color in patches, typically affecting the face, hands, and other exposed areas.

Warts

A condition that causes small, rough growths on the skin, often appearing on hands and feet.
